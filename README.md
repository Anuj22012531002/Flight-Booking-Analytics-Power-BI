# ✈️ Flight Booking Analytics | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze flight booking data, airline performance, revenue, pricing, customer behavior, and route-level insights.

The dashboard transforms raw flight booking data into meaningful KPIs and interactive visualizations to support data-driven analysis.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is divided into five analytical sections:

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of the flight booking business through key performance indicators and overall trends.

### 2. Airline Performance
Analyzes airline-wise performance and compares airlines based on bookings, revenue, pricing, and other business metrics.

### 3. Revenue & Pricing Analysis
Focuses on revenue generation, ticket pricing, fare trends, and pricing patterns across different segments.

### 4. Customer & Booking Analysis
Analyzes customer booking behavior, booking patterns, travel preferences, and other customer-related insights.

### 5. Route & Flight Analysis
Provides route-level and flight-level analysis to identify important travel routes, flight patterns, and operational trends.
